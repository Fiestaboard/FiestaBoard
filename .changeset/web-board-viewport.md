Board previews no longer shrink to fit: a board wider than its space keeps its real tiles and scrolls sideways inside its frame, with a fade on the side that has more board, snapping to each Note or column, a "Note 1 of 3" position readout, and ← → / Home / End keys. Fit (press F) is still there as an overview, and your choice lasts the session. Board sizes now read columns × rows ("45 × 3"). The editor shortcut list now includes these preview keys when the split-flap viewport is active.
