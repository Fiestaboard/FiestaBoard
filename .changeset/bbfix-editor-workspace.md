The desktop page editor is one workspace: the Explorer, the template with its Console and Timeline, and the preview with its Inspector sit in three columns under a header and toolbar that stay put while you scroll; a new page has the same layout as a saved one, so the first save moves nothing; Tab goes column by column; and the Explorer now shows only the open page's boards, parts and snippets, with no list of other pages.
