Schedules and collections can now be tested ahead of time: ask which page a display's schedule shows at any time of day, or which page a collection's rules would pick at a given time and with sample data, including a timeline of how its choice changes over the day. Rules that read the Date & Time plugin are answered for the time asked, and a timeline too costly to simulate in full says where it stopped.
