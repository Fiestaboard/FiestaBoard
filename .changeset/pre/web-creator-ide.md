The page editor becomes a Creator IDE on wide screens: an Explorer of pages and collections, a status row that says where the page is showing and whether your edits are a draft, Inspector tabs for Data, Logic, Page, Shows on and History, and a docked Problems / Warnings / Sent console with a week timeline. While a display shows the page you can Save draft or Save & update board, restore an earlier version into a draft, and make a copy sized for another display.
