The page editor keeps your unsaved edits to a saved page as a draft on the hub and brings them back when you return ("Draft restored · Discard"); the live page changes only when you press Save. The desktop header gains a mono meta line (type and size, display, live and save state), 40 px buttons and no Transition or Delete icons beside the title (Delete is in the Inspector's Page tab), and the leave dialog names the page and the lines you changed.
