Home is rebuilt on the canonical design: one "Compose a message" button in the page header, a Now showing card with Change Page, and both verbs ending in the same duration step (15 min, 1 hr, until the Schedule's next change, until you cancel) and Show now. An Override shows its badge, a countdown and what happens next, with Cancel override and Undo; every display gets its own timed override; and the board has a context menu (right-click, Shift+F10, long-press or the ⋯ button) with Change Page, Compose, Edit page, Copy as text, Refresh and Pause.
